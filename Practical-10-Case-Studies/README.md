# Practical 10 - Data Privacy Case Studies

## Aim
To analyze privacy incidents and identify lessons for prevention and response.

## Case studies
1. Equifax data breach
2. Cambridge Analytica / Facebook data controversy
3. Marriott data breach

For each case:
- What happened?
- What data was involved?
- What were the privacy/security impacts?
- What controls could have reduced the risk?
- What lessons can organizations learn?
