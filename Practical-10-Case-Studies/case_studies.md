# Data Privacy Case Studies

## Case 1 - Equifax
### What happened?
A major breach exposed personal information from a large number of consumers.

### Lessons
- Patch management is critical.
- Asset inventory and vulnerability management matter.
- Sensitive data requires strong access controls and monitoring.
- Incident response must be prepared before an incident.

## Case 2 - Cambridge Analytica / Facebook
### What happened?
Personal data associated with Facebook users was obtained through an application and used in ways that generated major privacy and consent concerns.

### Lessons
- Third-party application access needs strict controls.
- Data sharing should be transparent and purpose-limited.
- User consent and expectations matter.
- Platform APIs should minimize unnecessary data access.

## Case 3 - Marriott
### What happened?
A long-running security incident affected guest information.

### Lessons
- Monitoring and detection are essential.
- Organizations should understand inherited risks during acquisitions.
- Sensitive customer information needs layered security.

## Conclusion
Case studies demonstrate that privacy protection depends on governance, security controls, data minimization, monitoring, transparency and effective incident response.
