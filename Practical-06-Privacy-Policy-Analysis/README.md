# Practical 06 - Privacy Policy Analysis

## Aim
To analyze a privacy policy and identify how an organization explains collection, use, sharing, retention and user rights.

## Procedure
1. Select a publicly available privacy policy.
2. Identify categories of information collected.
3. Identify purposes of processing.
4. Check third-party sharing statements.
5. Check retention/security statements.
6. Check user choices/rights.
7. Record gaps or unclear wording.

## Result
The analysis should clearly separate what the policy states from your own evaluation.
