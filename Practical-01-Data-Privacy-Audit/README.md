# Practical 01 - Data Privacy Audit

## Aim
To conduct a basic data privacy audit of a sample organization and identify privacy risks.

## Objectives
- Identify categories of personal/sensitive data.
- Identify where data is collected, stored, shared and retained.
- Assign risk levels.
- Suggest mitigation controls.

## Procedure
1. List data assets.
2. Identify the purpose of collection.
3. Identify storage and access controls.
4. Check sharing and retention practices.
5. Score each risk.
6. Recommend controls.

## Result
The audit produces a risk register showing privacy risks and recommended controls.

## Conclusion
A privacy audit helps an organization identify weaknesses before they become privacy incidents.
